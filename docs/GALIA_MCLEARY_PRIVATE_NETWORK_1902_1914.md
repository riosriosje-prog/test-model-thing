## 1. Core title chain — finca 2109, Machuchal

**King v. Fernández et al., 30 D.P.R. 592 (1922)** reconstructs the registry
history of parcels in the northern section of Machuchal, Santurce.

The opinion states that:

- Finca No. **2109**, approximately **10 cuerdas**, was formed by segregation
  from a larger tract and acquired by **Margarida y Compañía** from
  **Raimundo Palacios**.
- Its first independent registry entry dates to **14 October 1902**.
- A parcel later identified as finca No. **2465** was segregated by Margarida
  y Cía. and sold to **Libertad Torres Grau** on **31 August 1904**.
- Torres Grau sold that parcel to **Charles A. Catlin** on
  **28 February 1905**.
- Another parcel, later finca No. **2599**, was segregated from finca 2109
  and sold by Margarida y Cía. to Catlin on **15 February 1906**.
- The registered description of the 600 m² parcel later associated with
  finca 2465 identifies, on its west boundary, **“una calle abierta por dicha
  sociedad en la misma finca.”**
- On **1 July 1914**, Margarida y Cía. sold Anastasio Quiñones a
  **1,587.50 m²** tract bounded:
  - north: Margarida & Co.;
  - south: Charles A. Catlin;
  - east: **Henry W. Dooley**;
  - west: Margarida & Co.
- The Quiñones title was entered on **20 July 1914**.

Source:
King v. Fernández et al., 30 D.P.R. 592 (1922), title-chain discussion of
Machuchal parcels and finca 2109.

Accessible text:
https://vlex.com.pr/vid/30-d-p-r-684621073

## 2. Independent Dooley title evidence

**Dooley v. Registrar of Property, 12 P.R. 202 (1907)** independently
documents that **Eliza B. K. Dooley**, with Henry W. Dooley's assent, acquired
a lot in **Machuchal, Santurce**, from **Fred E. Burnett** by public deed dated
**16 July 1906**.

The registry controversy identifies:
- San Juan registry;
- folio 41 reverse, volume 50;
- **estate/finca No. 2126**;
- registrar notation dated **27 November 1906**.

This independently confirms that the Dooley interests were already embedded
in the same Machuchal land market years before the 1914 tract description
places Henry W. Dooley on the eastern boundary.

Accessible text:
https://openjurist.org/12/pr/202/dooley-v-registrar-of-property

## 5. Eponym context — separate claim

James Harvey MacLeary served as an associate justice of the Supreme Court of
Puerto Rico, left Puerto Rico because of ill health in **November 1913**, and
died in Washington, D.C., on **5 January 1914**.

On **8 January 1914**, *The Times* of San Juan reported that a special meeting
of the Bar Association of the Federal Court would be held to pass resolutions
on his death. That establishes an immediate local memorialization context.

However, the MacLeary family also had continuing Puerto Rico connections.
Accordingly:

```text
EPONYM_CANDIDATE_JAMES_H_MACLEARY = PLAUSIBLE
EPONYM_IDENTITY                   = UNRESOLVED
```

The death chronology is consistent with a posthumous commemorative naming,
but it is not proof of one.

## 8. Geometric decomposition of the opened-street network

The registry descriptions reproduced in **King v. Fernández et al.,
30 D.P.R. 592 (1922)** require GALIA to model more than one road axis.

### Parcel 2599 — Catlin, 1906

The registered parcel is described as approximately:

- **north:** 47.70 m, sea;
- **south:** 92 m, **a street**;
- **east:** 160 m, Ceferino del Valle;
- **west:** 131.50 m, **a street and Libertad Torres Grau**.

This establishes one street on the south side and at least a partial street
edge on the west side of the large Catlin tract.

### Parcel 2465 — Torres Grau / Catlin, 1904–1905

The 600 m² parcel is described as:

- **north:** sea;
- **south and east:** Margarida & Co.;
- **west:** **a street opened by Margarida & Co. within the same parent
  estate**.

Because the west boundary is explicitly a street, this is a transverse
north–south-type axis relative to the recorded cardinal boundaries.

### 270 m² parcel derived from the 1914 Quiñones tract

The later parcel is described as:

- **north:** 10 m on **an opened street bordering the sea**;
- **south:** Charles A. Catlin;
- **east:** Concepción Tudela;
- **west:** **an opened street in lands of Margarida & Co.**

This description independently requires two distinct street edges meeting
around the parcel: a northern/coastal edge and a western/transverse edge.

## 9. 1917 map overlay target

The **General Plan of Santurce**, first section dated 30 August 1917 and held
through the Archivo General de Puerto Rico/Archivo Digital Nacional, is the
next map gate. It post-dates both:

- the 1 July 1914 Quiñones segregation; and
- the 22 November 1915 verified MacLeary newspaper designation.

Therefore it can serve as a geometry bridge between the title descriptions
and the named street network, but it cannot by itself prove the original
naming act.

- location of Taft;

  Margarida / Quiñones parcels;
